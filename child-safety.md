# Child Safety Standards – JobLink Portal

JobLink Portal is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and strictly prohibits any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).

## Prohibited Content
- Any content involving sexual exploitation or abuse of minors
- Grooming or sexual communication with minors
- Sharing or requesting CSAE-related material

## Safety Measures
- JobLink Portal is intended for users aged 18 and above
- Users can report abusive or suspicious behavior
- Violations result in immediate action, including account removal
